Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between modern and Italian wardrobes?

Modern wardrobes tend to feature minimal lines and neutral finishes, while Italian-style wardrobes often emphasize refined detailing, premium materials and elegant door profiles. We can help you choose by showing samples.


What is a modular wardrobe?

A modular wardrobe is made of standard-width sections that can be combined, extended or reconfigured, which makes it flexible and easier to adapt over time.


Can wardrobe doors be customized?

Yes. Doors come in sliding, hinged, mirrored, glass and solid options, with a choice of colors, textures and handle styles.


What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash and credit cards, and we review the cost with you before the project begins.

Italian Wardrobes

Italian-style wardrobes are known for proportion and detail. Slim frames, glass or lacquer-like finishes and refined hardware create a polished, high-end look.

We help you select materials and styles that fit your bedroom, and we build the interior with the same care as the doors. The result is a wardrobe that feels special every time you open it.

Modular Wardrobes

Modular wardrobes are made from sections that fit together neatly. You can begin with a compact arrangement and add sections later, or rearrange the interior as your needs evolve.

This approach is practical for renters, growing families and anyone who values flexibility. Even so, the finished result looks seamless and solid, not temporary.
